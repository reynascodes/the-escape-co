The mountains
on foot.

The Kanchenjunga approach is one of the great walks of the Himalaya. We run it slowly, with permits, camp crews and enough acclimatisation to actually enjoy the altitude.

TREKS WE RUN

Pick your altitude.

Both treks start from Yuksom. Dzongri is the shorter introduction; Goechala goes further, higher and closer to the wall.

What's included

  • National park permits
  • Camping and kitchen crew
  • All meals on trek
  • Local trek leader
  • Transfers from Siliguri
  • Oxygen and medical kit

When to come

March to May for rhododendrons and clear ridges. September to November for the sharpest mountain views and stable weather. We don't run high treks through the monsoon.

How fit do I need to be?

If you can walk five to six hours uphill with a day pack and recover overnight, you can train for this. We'll send a fitness plan when you book — and a leader who knows when to turn around.

Packing, honestly

What people forget.

Layers beat one thick jacket. Two litres of water capacity, not one. A headlamp you actually tested. And a dry bag, because everything in the Himalaya eventually gets wet.
