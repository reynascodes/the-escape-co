The Escape Co. · Sikkim

We believe the best stories begin where the road ends.

The Escape Co. was built around a simple idea: travel should make you feel alive.

Rooted in the mountains of Sikkim, we bring together adventure, exploration and meaningful travel experiences. From navigating winding Himalayan roads to trekking remote mountain trails, we create opportunities to experience the natural beauty and cultural richness of the regions we explore.

We started because the trips being sold in this part of the world didn't look like the trips we were taking. A hotel, a vehicle and a list of sightseeing points is not a journey. The drive between two destinations isn't dead time — on a motorcycle, it's the whole point.

So we build journeys the other way round: start with the experience, then work out the route, the stays, the pace and the permits around it.

We believe in discovering places beyond the usual tourist routes, connecting with local communities and making every journey personal. Our goal is to build a community of explorers who value experiences over itineraries and memories over milestones.
