How we ride

Support, not chaperoning.

A support vehicle follows the group carrying luggage, spares and oxygen. A lead rider sets the pace, a sweep rider makes sure nobody is alone at the back. You ride; we handle the rest.

  • Custom riding routes

  • Permit assistance
  • Support vehicle and mechanic
  • Fuel for the route
  • Riding gear available on request

Before you book

The honest version.

Experience

Hill riding experience matters. If you've only ridden in a city, start with the North Bengal circuit before attempting 17,800 ft.

Altitude

The bike doesn't get altitude sickness. You do. We build acclimatisation into the route and carry oxygen on every high day.

Weather

Mountain roads close. Permits get refused. We reroute and explain why — that's part of the deal, not a failure.
